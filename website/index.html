The problem

Every router speaks a different language

Ubiquiti wants files dropped into /config. MikroTik wants CLI commands one at a time. TP-Link hides everything behind its web forms. So the person who manages the network ends up keeping a notes app full of half-remembered incantations.

RouterConfig Pro speaks all three. You describe what the network should be, and it applies the vendor-correct changes over that device's own SSH interface — with a real terminal underneath, so nothing is ever out of reach.

Why it exists

Built by someone who runs the gear

I got tired of four browsers open, four different config formats, and one wrong line taking down a customer. So I built a tool that treats the whole fleet as one system: discover what's there, configure it correctly, deploy it, and if something breaks — tunnel straight in and fix it by hand.

It's a serious tool for people who manage real networks. Data stays on your machine, credentials are stored encrypted, and there's no cloud account to argue with.

— Shahid

Your Privacy
