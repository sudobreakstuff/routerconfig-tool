What is RouterConfig Pro?

It's the tool for the person who has to make 40 customer routers behave. You point it at a network, it discovers the gear, and it walks the right config onto every device using that vendor's own SSH interface — EdgeOS files for Ubiquiti, CLI commands for MikroTik, LUCI/forms for TP-Link. No copy-pasting blocks of CLI from a notes app, no guessing which template fits which router.

Built around the Jenny Internet CPE workflow: a new customer router gets auto-configured, verified, and pushed to the ISP inventory in a single Deploy action. Field technicians get a live terminal and direct tunnels into downstream devices; the NOC gets bulk operations and health checks across the fleet.

Everything runs on your machine. Device credentials are stored encrypted, the API is protected by a per-install token, and nothing leaves your network unless you push it to the ISP inventory yourself.

Why I built it

Because configuring routers for a real ISP means fighting four different vendors' quirks every single day. One template fits Ubiquiti but not MikroTik; one wrong CLI line bricks a customer session; and the person in the field has to phone the office to run a command they can't reach. I wanted the whole workflow — discover, configure, deploy, diagnose, tunnel — in one honest, local-first tool that knows each vendor's language.

RouterConfig Pro is a serious tool, not a script collection. It keeps your data encrypted and local, gives you a real terminal into any device, and automates the boring parts so you can do the work in minutes instead of an afternoon.

— Shahid

The Mission

ISP work shouldn't be a wall of copied CLI. Anyone deploying real networks deserves software that knows each vendor's language and does the boring parts automatically.

Your Privacy

Everything runs on your machine with no telemetry. Credentials are encrypted on disk; data only leaves your network when you explicitly push a deployment to the ISP.

Local-first

The whole backend runs on your own computer. There's no cloud to trust, no monthly bill, no account to create — just install and work.

Your Stack

Add your own ISP upload adapter or device driver. RouterConfig Pro is designed to be extended for whatever network you run.
